## Overview
This project is a Excel dashboard that tracks key metrics for a coffee shop, helping business owners analyze sales, product performance, footfall, and customer behavior. It provides insights into the performance of the coffee shop over different time periods, days of the week, and locations, enabling better decision-making.

 ## Sample Data: A structured dataset with columns such as:
* Date
* Time
* Product Name
* Category
* Store Location
* Order Size
* Sales Amount
* Quantity Sold
* Customer ID

## Features:
**Total Sales**: Displays the total sales generated by the coffee shop.
**Order Quantity Based on Hours**: Visualizes the quantity of orders throughout different hours of the day, helping identify peak times.
**Sales by Weekdays and Weekends**: A comparison of sales between weekdays and weekends to understand consumer behavior patterns.
**Top 5 Products Based on Transaction Count**: A breakdown of the top-selling products by transaction volume.
**Top 5 Products Based on Sales**: Shows the products generating the highest revenue.
**Size Distribution of Orders**: Displays the size breakdown (e.g., Large, Small, Regular, Not Defined) for coffee orders.
**Footfall and Sales Across Multiple Locations**: Tracks the footfall and sales for different store locations.
**Additional KPIs**: Includes average bill per person and average order per person.

## Data Insights:
**The dashboard shows sales trends, product performance, and customer preferences**.
**Sales by Hour**: Insights into peak sales hours help optimize staff allocation and inventory management.
**Sales by Day**: Identifies the most profitable days of the week to tailor marketing and promotions.
**Footfall and Sales by Location**: Helps evaluate store performance and decide on resource allocation across locations.
